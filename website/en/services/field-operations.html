Field Air Packages — Turnkey Operation

Compressors, booster, line equipment and certified operators: we take over the entire air system at the wellhead under a single contract. You focus on drilling — compressed air is our job.

One responsible party, zero mismatch

Air spreads assembled from multiple suppliers fail in predictable ways: the booster's inlet range doesn't match the feed, flows don't add up, and when something breaks each party points at the other. With a turnkey package that risk simply doesn't exist — the whole system is our engineering and our responsibility.

The package is sized to your well program, rigged up at site, run in shifts by our operators and demobilized when the well is done. Fuel management, maintenance planning and spare-parts logistics are all on us.

  • Flow-and-pressure sizing and equipment selection
  • Primary compressor(s) + booster + manifold and line equipment
  • Certified operators on 12/24-hour shift rotation
  • Optional foam/mist injection unit
  • Daily operation report (hours, pressure/flow log, fuel)
  • Fast demobilization at total depth
