Booster Rental

Where primary compressor pressure runs out, we step in: field-proven boosters delivering up to 155 bar (2,250 psi) — transport, commissioning and 24/7 support included.

Why rent instead of buy?

A high-pressure booster is a serious capital item that few drilling contractors can keep utilized year-round. With rental, the investment, depreciation, maintenance staff and parts inventory all stay on our side — you pay only for the duration your well program actually needs.

And every project is different: the unit that covers a 1,200 m water well is undersized for a 2,800 m geothermal well. Renting lets you match the right unit to every hole instead of being locked into one fixed asset.

  • Daily, monthly and per-project rental models
  • Transport, rig-up and commissioning included
  • Pre-mobilization full load test with written report
  • Critical spares kit travels with the unit
  • Optional certified operator
  • Volume discounts on long-term rentals

FAQ

Common questions

What is the minimum rental period?
Standard minimum is 7 days. Daily rental is available for tests and short operations, with mobilization priced separately.
Is a booster enough on its own?
No — a booster does not generate air; it takes 250–500 psi feed air from primary compressors and multiplies the pressure. If you don't have primaries, consider our turnkey air package.
Who supplies fuel?
Fuel is on the client in the standard contract. An all-inclusive rate with fuel is available on request — useful for budget predictability on long projects.
Do you mobilize internationally?
Yes. We regularly export equipment and crews to the Middle East, Central Asia and North Africa; we handle customs and logistics planning and share reference mobilization timelines at quote stage.

Which booster fits your well?

Send depth, hole sizes and formation; we'll run the sizing and quote the same day.
