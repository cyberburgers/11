Why Us

It doesn't end with selling the part

The real difference in parts supply is correct diagnosis: was it the valve, the rings, or a cooling problem upstream? Because our parts desk sits next to our service team, we pre-diagnose by phone before you order — and prevent the wrong-part round trip.

  • Correct part identification from the serial number
  • Stock orders placed by 3 PM ship the same day
  • Emergency courier delivery to site
  • Optional service crew for parts requiring installation
  • Consignment stock model for fleet customers

What we need for your order

  1. Equipment make and model (e.g. Hurricane B12-48/1500)
  2. Serial number (a photo of the nameplate is best)
  3. Part name or OEM part number if available
  4. Failure symptoms (for pre-diagnosis)
  5. Delivery address and urgency
Tip: When mobilizing a unit to a new site, order the critical consumables kit (filters + separator + valve gaskets) with it — don't burn rig hours waiting for a courier.
