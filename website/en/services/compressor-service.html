Service

Compressor & Booster Service

If you run your own equipment, your most critical partner is your service shop. From planned maintenance to complete overhauls, from airend rebuilds to emergency field calls — drilling compressors are our specialty.

Scope

What we do

Planned Maintenance Contracts

Hour-based maintenance schedules with OEM or approved parts. Contract customers get priority breakdown response and discounted labor.

Complete Overhauls

Engine and airend included: teardown, measurement and reporting, parts renewal, corrosion protection, load test and delivery with warranty certificate.

Airend Rebuilds

Bearing and seal renewal, rotor inspection and balancing for screw compressor airends — extending unit life where replacement isn't economical.

Booster Valve & Piston Groups

Valve kits, rings, liners and bearings on piston boosters; intercooler/aftercooler cleaning and pressure testing.

24/7 Breakdown Response

Mobile service trucks with diagnostics and critical parts stock. Resident crews in geothermal regions respond same-day to most calls.

Oil & Vibration Analysis

Periodic sampling and vibration surveys catch failures before they happen; reports are archived digitally with your unit history.
