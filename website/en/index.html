Why Boreas Air

The hidden cost in every well: air that stops

When the compressor or booster goes down, the rig goes down with it — and rig time costs a multiple of any equipment rate. That's why we define our job not as "supplying equipment" but as guaranteeing uninterrupted compressed air at the wellhead.

  • Full load test with report before every mobilization
  • Critical spare parts kit travels with every unit
  • Certified, experienced compressor/booster operators
  • Free flow-and-pressure sizing for your well program
  • 24/7 phone support, ≤24 h field response commitment
