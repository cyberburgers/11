Company

A team that comes from the field

Boreas Air was founded by people who came up through the drilling business — compressor operators, service engineers, field supervisors. We don't just supply compressed air; we spent years working next to it at the wellhead.

Our Story

A trade learned at the wellhead

The core of our team has been running compressor and booster operations on Turkey's geothermal and water-well sites since the early 2000s. Over the years we watched the same story repeat: wrongly sized equipment arriving at site, breakdowns waiting days for a response, rig hours burning for nothing.

Boreas Air was founded as the answer. We put the well program — not the equipment — at the center of the job: first calculate the need correctly, then send the right unit, then keep it running without interruption.

Today we operate from our Ankara workshop and resident Aegean field crews across Turkey, with project work in the Middle East, Central Asia and North Africa.

How we work

Correct math first

The well program speaks, not the sales target. If a smaller unit covers your need, we won't push the bigger one; if the package won't be enough, we say so up front.

Commitments in writing

Response times go into the contract and get honored. We don't promise deadlines we can't keep.

Safety is non-negotiable

High-pressure air carries serious energy. Whip-checks, certified hoses and pressure tests are standard on every mobilization.

Transparent reporting

Daily operation reports on manned packages; photographic work reports with measured values on every service job.

Let's work your next well together

Reference projects and client contacts are shared on request.
